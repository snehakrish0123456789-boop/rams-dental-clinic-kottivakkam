1. How often should I visit the dentist?

Every 6 months for regular checkups and cleanings, though some patients may need more frequent visits.

2. How can I maintain good oral hygiene at home?

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, floss once a day and limit sugary snacks.

3. Are dental treatments painful?

Modern techniques and local anaesthesia keep most treatments comfortable. Talk to the doctor about any worries.

4. How long does a dental implant last?

With good care, implants can last many years. Your dentist can advise on your case.
